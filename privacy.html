Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Ginger is a cooking assistant you chat with on WhatsApp. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number, so we know who we're talking to and can keep your preferences.
  • Messages you send Ginger, such as ingredients and requests.
  • Photos you send, such as a picture of your fridge. Photos are used to suggest recipes and are not kept after your reply is created.
  • Cooking preferences you share, such as "we're vegetarian" or "no mushrooms", plus a short history of recent messages and meals so suggestions don't repeat.

Please don't send sensitive personal information (like health or financial details) to Ginger.

How we use it

  • To reply with cooking ideas and recipes.
  • To remember your preferences so future suggestions fit your household.
  • To keep the service running and improve it.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

To run Ginger, your messages pass through these service providers:

  • WhatsApp (Meta) and Twilio, to send and receive messages.
  • Anthropic (Claude AI), to generate cooking suggestions from your message or photo.
  • Render (hosting) and Neon (database), to run the service and store your preferences.

How long we keep it

We keep your saved preferences and recent message history while you use Ginger. We delete them when you ask us to.

Your choices

  • Stop at any time by simply not messaging Ginger.
  • Delete your data by emailing YOUR-EMAIL@example.com from or with the phone number you use. We'll delete your saved preferences and history.

Important

Ginger's suggestions are ideas, not dietary, nutritional or medical advice. AI can make mistakes. Always check ingredients for allergies and follow safe food handling.

Contact

Questions? Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
